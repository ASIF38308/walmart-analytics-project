# 1. Executive Summary

Walmart India currently generates large volumes of operational and transactional data across Sales, Marketing, Inventory, Customers, Finance, and Supply Chain. However, reporting is fragmented across departments, resulting in inconsistent business metrics, manual reporting efforts, and delayed decision-making.

This project aims to establish a centralized Business Intelligence platform that consolidates data from multiple business functions into a single source of truth. The platform will provide standardized KPIs, interactive dashboards, and analytical insights to support strategic and operational decision-making across the organization.

---

# 2. Business Problem

The organization currently faces several business challenges:

* Reporting is performed manually by different departments.
* Revenue figures differ across business teams.
* Marketing campaigns lack standardized performance tracking.
* Inventory teams experience both stock-outs and overstock situations.
* Customer purchasing behavior is not being analyzed effectively.
* Business leaders lack a centralized view of organizational performance.

These issues reduce operational efficiency and delay data-driven decision-making.

---

# 3. Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

* Build a centralized analytics platform.
* Establish a single source of truth for business reporting.
* Standardize KPIs across departments.
* Improve reporting accuracy and consistency.
* Reduce manual reporting effort.
* Enable faster executive decision-making.
* Provide actionable insights for Sales, Marketing, Inventory, Product, and Customer teams.

---

# 4. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

* Business teams access a centralized analytics platform.
* Reports provide consistent business metrics across departments.
* Manual reporting effort is significantly reduced.
* Executive dashboards support faster decision-making.
* Marketing campaign performance can be measured using standardized KPIs.
* Inventory visibility improves through centralized reporting.
* Business users can independently access required dashboards and reports.

---

# 5. Project Scope

## In Scope

The project includes:

* Sales Analytics
* Marketing Analytics
* Customer Analytics
* Inventory Analytics
* Product Analytics
* Executive KPI Dashboard
* SQL-based business analysis
* Tableau dashboards
* KPI framework development
* Business documentation
* Root Cause Analysis
* User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

## Out of Scope

The following items are excluded from this phase:

* ERP implementation
* Mobile application development
* Machine Learning models
* Predictive AI solutions
* Cloud infrastructure deployment
* Payment gateway development
* HR analytics
* Customer support systems
