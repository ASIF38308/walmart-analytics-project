# Meeting Minutes

## Marketing Analytics Requirements Discussion

**Project:** Walmart Retail Analytics & Business Intelligence Platform

**Meeting Type:** Stakeholder Requirement Gathering

**Stakeholder:** Marketing Head

**Business Analyst:** Mohd Asif

**Meeting Status:** Completed

**Date:** August 2026

---

## 1. Meeting Objective

To understand the Marketing team's current reporting process, business challenges, analytical requirements, KPIs, data requirements, and expected capabilities of the centralized marketing analytics solution.

---

## 2. Business Need

The Marketing team currently spends significant time manually consolidating campaign data from multiple marketing channels.

The Marketing Head requires a centralized analytics solution that provides reliable and standardized campaign performance information to support:

* Marketing budget allocation
* Campaign optimization
* Campaign scaling or reduction
* Historical campaign comparison
* Identification of underperforming campaigns and channels

---

## 3. Current Process (As-Is)

The current reporting process is largely manual.

```text
Marketing Channels
        ↓
Download Individual Reports
        ↓
Excel Consolidation
        ↓
Manual Data Cleaning
        ↓
Manual KPI Calculation
        ↓
Finance Data Added Separately
        ↓
Weekly Marketing Report
        ↓
Marketing Head
```

---

## 4. Current Pain Points

The Marketing Head identified the following challenges:

1. Marketing data is fragmented across multiple platforms.
2. Reports require significant manual Excel processing.
3. KPI definitions are not consistently applied across Marketing and Finance.
4. Weekly reporting takes approximately 1–2 working days.
5. Ad-hoc analysis requires manual modification of Excel files.
6. Historical campaign comparison is difficult.
7. Manual processes create potential data-quality and human-error risks.
8. Analysts spend significant time preparing reports rather than generating insights.

---

## 5. Business Decisions Supported

The analytics solution should support decisions related to:

* Marketing budget allocation
* Campaign scaling
* Campaign reduction or discontinuation
* Channel performance evaluation
* Campaign performance comparison
* Historical performance analysis

## 7. Historical Data Requirement

The initial solution should include campaign data from:

**January 2024 onward**

The Marketing Head confirmed that January 2024 should be used as the starting point rather than requiring exactly three complete years.

Older reliable data may be considered as a future enhancement.

## 11. Performance Benchmarks

The Marketing Head provided an initial CAC benchmark of:

**₹500**

However, this should be treated as a **baseline benchmark**, not a universal target.

CAC expectations may vary depending on:

* Campaign objective
* Marketing channel
* Customer type
* Campaign type

Formal channel-specific and campaign-specific CAC targets have not yet been established.

---

## 12. Underperformance Definition

Initial guidelines discussed:

* ROAS below 2.0 may indicate underperformance.
* CAC significantly above the applicable benchmark may indicate underperformance.
* Conversion Rate below 2% may indicate potential concern.

However, these thresholds should not be applied blindly across all campaign types.

Campaign objective and type must be considered when evaluating performance.

---
